Our game plan

Stretch them. Get in behind.

Midfield & wingers Wingers hold the width. Get close: within 8m of the ball. Set and spin. Ball in behind into the channel. Press their 6. Shut the lane into 20 and 17. Lose it: 5 seconds to win it back.
